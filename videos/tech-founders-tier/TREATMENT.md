# Treatment — "Tech Founders Tier List"

A 26-second silent-read tier list built for scrolling with the sound off, with a music bed and
pop/whoosh effects for viewers who have sound on.

- **Open (0–2 s):** big "TECH FOUNDERS / TIER LIST" title over an empty S–D board.
- **Nine verdicts (2.4 s each):** the founder's tile pops onto a centre stage, their name slams in
  above, a one-line roast and the coloured tier verdict sit below. In the last half-second the text
  fades and the tile glides down into its row. The board keeps every earlier placement, so it fills up.
- **Order for tension:** start at the bottom (D), bounce through the middle, save two S-tiers for last
  (Jensen Huang, then Steve Jobs as the final word).
- **Close (23.6–26.4 s):** "Who did I rank wrong?" with "Fight me in the comments" over the full board.

Look: near-black background, the classic red→green tier colours, Montserrat Black for names,
Inter Bold for lines. Tiles are brand-colour gradients with initials and a name strip.
Audio: the Hypit example's shared soundtrack at low gain plus its ranking appear/move effects.
